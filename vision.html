Vision & Mission

Vision

To build a safer, secure, and disaster-resilient world by fostering innovation, empowering communities, and promoting sustainable, collective action — integrating disaster risk reduction into governance, public policy, and everyday life.

Mission

At DMICS, our mission is to inspire, innovate, and implement solutions that reduce disaster risks and build resilient communities. We believe disaster management must be proactive and deeply embedded in governance, public policy, and everyday community practices.

To achieve this, we aim to:

  • Encourage and promote new and organic ideas that reduce vulnerabilities, strengthen institutional frameworks, and influence public policy.
  • Create a platform for convergence among government agencies, civil society, academia, and communities for effective Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR).
  • Integrate disaster management into governance and public policy, making resilience a core pillar of sustainable development.
  • Foster people’s participation, local leadership, and community-driven solutions through synergy and collective action.
  • Organize global events, campaigns, and knowledge exchanges to share best practices, innovations, and indigenous knowledge.
  • Pursue disaster management initiatives with dedication to build a resilient, sustainable, and inclusive world.
