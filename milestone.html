The Journey of DMICS

The Journey of DMICS

Milestones & Evolution

Disaster Management Initiatives and Convergence Society (DMICS): Championing a Resilient Future

Founded in 2005 in response to the devastating Indian Ocean tsunami, the Disaster Management Initiatives and Convergence Society (DMICS) is committed to advancing the understanding of disaster risks and promoting mitigation strategies. Our mission focuses on enhancing preparedness, response, and recovery through interdisciplinary research, training, and collaborative engagement with a diverse range of stakeholders.

DMICS: Bridging Knowledge and Action

DMICS is a vital hub, bringing together experts, scientists, policymakers, NGOs, INGOs, academicians, the media, public and private sectors, and practitioners from various fields. We aim to foster collaboration and knowledge-sharing to build a safer, disaster-resilient world. We provide tailored services to a wide range of stakeholders, including governments and corporate entities, guiding them through the complexities of disaster management.

DMICS: Driving Positive Change

With a dedicated team of global experts, professionals, and practitioners, DMICS leads the transformation of disaster risk reduction and management. Through its commitment to knowledge sharing, collaboration, and innovation, DMICS unites stakeholders, fosters partnerships, and promotes cutting-edge solutions. By leveraging technology and innovation, DMICS is playing a crucial role in building a more resilient and secure world for all.

World Congress on Disaster Management (WCDM): A Premier Global Forum

The World Congress on Disaster Management (WCDM), DMICS's flagship event, is a globally renowned platform for knowledge exchange and collaboration. Jointly organized with various state governments, each edition is inaugurated by chief ministers and union cabinet ministers of India. Since its inaugural event in 2008, opened/chaired by former President Dr. A P J Abdul Kalam, WCDM has evolved into a leading forum for disaster management, attracting thousands of international delegates.

Key Highlights

  • Legacy of Success: Six WCDM editions have been held across India, with the 7th edition scheduled for Goa in 2025.
  • Global Engagement: Increasing participation from global experts and practitioners at each WCDM.
  • Scientific Excellence: Peer-reviewed research papers presented at WCDM are published by prestigious international publishers.
  • DRR Awards: DMICS has launched annual awards to recognize exceptional contributions in disaster risk reduction policies/frameworks.
  • Current Initiatives: Ongoing projects include a nationwide school safety program and seismic safety improvements in Northeast India.
