Why Volunteer With DMICS?

  • National & Global Network: Engage directly in flagship platforms like the World Congress on Disaster Management (WCDM).
  • Capacity Building: Receive hands-on orientation and training on disaster risk management and emergency preparedness.
  • Official Recognition: Earn certificates of service and letters of appreciation for verified humanitarian field contribution.
  • Community Impact: Directly contribute towards building resilient, disaster-prepared villages and cities across India.
