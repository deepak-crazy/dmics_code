International Collaborations

Global Partnerships & Multilateral Alliances

International Collaborations

View National Collaborations

DMICS actively collaborates with United Nations bodies, regional governance networks, international NGOs, and global scientific institutes. Through these strategic partnerships, DMICS facilitates global knowledge transfers, implements international disaster management frameworks, and builds community-centred disaster resilience worldwide.

Global Partner Organizations

UNISDR / UNDRR

United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction

Global focal point in the UN system for DRR and coordinator of Sendai Framework implementation, disaster monitoring, and multi-hazard resilience agendas.

Global UN Entity

UNICEF

United Nations Children’s Fund

Advancing child-centred disaster risk reduction, school safety protocols, emergency child protection, and hygiene access in disaster-hit communities.

UN Specialized Agency

NAPSIPAG

Network of Asia-Pacific Schools of Public Administration

Regional academic and governance consortium dedicated to capacity enhancement, policy integration, and disaster governance research.

Asia-Pacific Network

Oxfam

International Humanitarian Organization

Global confederation delivering humanitarian relief, poverty reduction, clean water infrastructure, and community vulnerability mitigation.

International NGO

UNFPA

United Nations Population Fund

Safeguarding dignity, reproductive health, and emergency support for women, adolescent girls, and marginalized groups during climate and disaster shocks.

UN Specialized Agency

Partner With DMICS

DMICS welcomes international research consortia, UN initiatives, and disaster resilience foundations for joint academic conferences, DRR awards, and capacity building.
