Strategic Plan

Organizational Roadmap · 7 Strategic Pillars

Driving Convergence in Disaster Management

Strategic Framework

To achieve its vision and mission, DMICS follows a focused strategic plan built around the following pillars:

Pillar 1

Innovation and Research

Promote cutting-edge research, indigenous knowledge systems, and innovative practices to enhance disaster risk reduction and resilience-building efforts.

Pillar 2

Policy Advocacy and Integration

Advocate for the integration of disaster management and climate resilience into public policy, governance frameworks, and development planning.

Pillar 3

Capacity Building and Empowerment

Strengthen the capabilities of communities, institutions, and frontline workers through training programs, workshops, and knowledge-sharing platforms.

Pillar 4

Community Engagement and Participation

Prioritize local leadership, community-driven solutions, and participatory approaches in disaster preparedness, mitigation, and recovery.

Strategic Architecture

Strategic Framework & Implementation Model

Official DMICS Strategic Framework aligning grassroots community action with multi-sectoral convergence.

Pillar 5

Collaborative Networks and Partnerships

Foster national and international partnerships with governments, academia, civil society, and private sectors to create a convergence of ideas, expertise, and action.

Pillar 6

Knowledge Exchange and Global Events

Organize conferences, conclaves, and campaigns to disseminate best practices, innovations, and case studies in disaster management.

Pillar 7

Sustainability and Inclusive Development

Ensure all disaster resilience efforts align with sustainability goals, social inclusion, and the broader vision of building resilient and equitable communities.
