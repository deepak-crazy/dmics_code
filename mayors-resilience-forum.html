Convening Leaders

Mayors' Resilience Forum & Parliamentarians' CCA-DRR Caucus

Two standing platforms that bring political and civic leadership into the resilience conversation, where policy decisions and public investments on risk are ultimately made.

This multi-city standing forum is separate from the individual Mayoral Office portfolio. Visit Office of the Mayor & Engagement Programme
Municipal · Cities Platform
Cities

Mayors' Resilience Forum

A standing forum for city leaders to share tested practices, shape municipal policy, and drive urban resilience and climate adaptation at the local level.

Core Action Pillars:

  • Peer-to-Peer City Reviews: Bilateral and regional knowledge transfers on flood mitigation, drainage modernization, and heat mitigation.
  • Ward-Level Early Warning: Operational protocols connecting municipal command centers directly with ward councilors and vulnerable communities.
  • Resilient Municipal Financing: Structuring urban green bonds, calamity contingency funds, and private-sector CSR partnerships.
  • World Congress Mayoral Conclave: An annual high-level summit bringing urban local leaders together with Union and State ministers.

Legislators

Parliamentarians' CCA-DRR Caucus

A cross-party caucus advancing climate change adaptation and disaster risk reduction through legislation, oversight, progressive public budgeting, and advocacy.

Core Action Pillars:

  • Legislative Frameworks: Drafting model state disaster policies, zoning reforms, and environmental protection bills.
  • Budgetary Scrutiny: Advocating for dedicated, non-lapsable disaster mitigation and climate resilience capital expenditures.
  • Sendai & SDG Audits: Conducting periodic legislative hearings on the mid-term progress of India's Sendai commitments.
  • Cross-Party Parliamentary Dialogues: Bridging regional political divides to forge national consensus on disaster risk resilience.

The Strategic Imperative

Bridging Political Will with Ground Implementation

Technical research and scientific guidelines achieve their full potential only when municipal commissioners and elected legislators champion them. The Mayors' Resilience Forum and Parliamentarians' Caucus connect ground reality with policy clout.

Policy & Legal Backing

Ensuring city bylaws, construction norms, and regional master plans reflect modern disaster science.

Resource Allocation

Securing dedicated budgetary envelopes for pre-disaster mitigation rather than post-calamity relief alone.

Community Accountability

Holding local governance institutions accountable for safety standards and emergency response speed.
