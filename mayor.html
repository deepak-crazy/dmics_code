Structured Municipal Action

The Mayoral Engagement Programme

DMICS works collaboratively with municipal corporations, mayors, and urban local bodies through a three-pronged framework combining peer reviews, technical advisory, and high-level national policy dialogues.

Programme

Mayoral Engagement Programme

A structured programme of work with city leaders: resilience reviews, peer exchange between cities, and direct technical support in translating national frameworks into municipal action.

  • Peer city resilience reviews
  • Inter-municipal learning exchanges
  • Framework localization toolkits
Support

City Resilience Advisory

Hands-on advisory for municipal bodies on risk assessment, urban heat and flood action plans, early warning at ward level, and resilient infrastructure planning.

  • Ward-level risk mapping & audits
  • Urban heat & flood response plans
  • Drainage & resilient infrastructure
Recognition

Mayors' Roundtable at the Congress

A standing mayoral session at the World Congress, where city leaders present what has worked, and what has not, to their peers and to national policymakers.

  • Plenary representation at WCDM
  • Direct dialogue with Union Ministries
  • Mayoral Declaration on Urban Risk
