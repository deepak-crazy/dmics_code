Congress Declaration Dehradun

Adoption of the Landmark Dehradun Declaration at the 6th World Congress on Disaster Management

Over 2,000 global delegates and 200 international scientists concluded the 6th WCDM with the formal adoption of the Dehradun Declaration, calling for accelerated last-mile early warning coverage and institutionalization of mountain disaster resilience.

Government of Uttarakhand & DMICS Read Dehradun Declaration
City Resilience Municipal Platform

DMICS Launches Mayors' Resilience Forum to Accelerate Ward-Level Disaster Preparedness in 50 Indian Cities

Recognizing that mayors and municipal leaders sit closest to climate vulnerability, DMICS inaugurates a standing peer review platform for urban heat protocols, stormwater drainage retrofitting, and localized contingency funds.
